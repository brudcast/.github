![Brudcast: Reach Everyone, Every time](assets/banner.svg)

Brudcast is how businesses reach their customers on every channel: email, SMS, WhatsApp, push
notifications and in-app messages, from one platform. The SDKs and integrations that connect your
apps to it are open source, and they live here.

## Open source

**Mobile SDKs**: in-app messaging and push notifications

- [brudcast-sdk-ios](https://github.com/brudcast/brudcast-sdk-ios): Swift SDK for iOS apps
- [brudcast-sdk-android](https://github.com/brudcast/brudcast-sdk-android): Kotlin SDK for Android apps
- [brudcast-sdk-flutter](https://github.com/brudcast/brudcast-sdk-flutter): Flutter plugins over the native iOS and Android SDKs
- [brudcast-sample-backend](https://github.com/brudcast/brudcast-sample-backend): the server half of a mobile integration, for the sample apps

**Mail transports**: send through the Brudcast mailing service from the framework you already use

- [nodemailer-brudcast-transport](https://github.com/brudcast/nodemailer-brudcast-transport): Nodemailer transport for Node.js
- [laravel-brudcast-transport](https://github.com/brudcast/laravel-brudcast-transport): Laravel mail transport
- [wordpress-brudcast-transport](https://github.com/brudcast/wordpress-brudcast-transport): WordPress plugin that sends every `wp_mail()` email
